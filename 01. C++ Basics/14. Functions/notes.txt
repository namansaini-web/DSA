  # Inbuilt functions :

1. max() --> gives out the max number out of the two.
2. min() --> gives out the min number out of the two.
  // more then two arguments will give an error.  

3. pow() --> takes the second argument a power and 
            first as base.  
4. sqrt() --> gives the square root. 
5. cbrt() --> gives the cube root.
6. abs() --> gives the absolute value.
